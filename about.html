Inspired by STUMPS – The Cricket Scorer

Key cricket scoring concepts implemented in our browser app.

What STUMPS Does

  • Ball-by-Ball Scoring: Instant tap buttons for 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, Wides, No-Balls, and Wickets.
  • Automatic Over Roll-Over: Counts 6 legal deliveries per over while keeping extras extra.
  • Strike Rotation: Automatically swaps batter strike on odd runs (1, 3) and at the end of every over.
  • Undo Mistake Support: Reverts the last delivery cleanly if a scorer taps the wrong button.
